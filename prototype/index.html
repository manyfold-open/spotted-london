Built for the pavement

People find pop-ups while they are out, so the phone layout is the main one. Everything in the frame works.

  1. Bottom tabs instead of a hamburgerThe five things people do most are one thumb tap away. A hamburger hides them and they get used less.
  2. Add sits in the middlePosting is what keeps the platform alive, so it gets the raised button.
  3. Paste a link, AI fills the formInstagram, TikTok, Eventbrite or a screenshot. Blue fields came from AI and need a quick check.
  4. Map with swipeable cardsTap a pin and its card slides into view, like a maps app.
  5. Details open as a bottom sheetSave and Directions stay pinned at the bottom, where your thumb is.
  6. Less used things live in MeReview queue, newsletter, notifications and account settings.
